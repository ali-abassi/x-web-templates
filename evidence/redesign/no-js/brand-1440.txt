FURION / BRAND NOTES

Less said. More done.

Purpose
A performance-editorial concept about movement, intention, and showing up.
This is a reusable demonstration identity, not an operating sports company.

Color
Ink: #111111
Paper: #F4F3EE
Secondary text on ink: #B0B0A9

Typography
Nimbus Sans Bold: uppercase display, tight tracking, direct statements.
Nimbus Sans Regular: readable body text and quiet navigation.
Font notices are included in the template's licenses folder.

Imagery
Original ChatGPT-generated black-and-white runner photograph.
Keep the human subject in the frame. Keep type separate and editable.
No logos, fake product endorsements, or commercial claims in the image.

Principles
Movement before decoration.
One clear action per section.
Black and white, with texture from photography.
No animation required to understand the page.

Reuse
Edit the HTML copy, CSS color variables, and local assets.
Replace concept statements with accurate information for your organization.
