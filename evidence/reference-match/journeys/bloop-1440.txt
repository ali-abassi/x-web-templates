BLOOP / BRAND NOTES

tiny blob. big brain.

Palette
Lime #A3D33D
Olive #53751D
White #FFFFFF
Canvas #EEEEEE

Typography
Rubik Bubbles Regular for the scalloped display direction; Nimbus Sans for body.
These are open, locally served font analogues; exact reference font is unconfirmed.

Composition
Six wide identity panels: hero, palette, beginning, nature, applications, philosophy.
Desktop: two columns with generous gray gutters. Mobile: one column with readable copy.

Imagery
Original generated motion-blurred tree/sky and sunlit canopy photography.
Teal sky, yellow-green foliage, gentle analog texture.
